About

Hi, I'm Duy! 👋 I'm a third-year PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, advised by Prof. Minh N. Do and co-advised by Prof. Khoa D. Doan (VinUniversity). My research asks how to make multimodal and generative models dependable outside curated benchmarks: when a modality goes missing, when inputs are corrupted, when labels are scarce, or when a scene holds many people at once.

My path into AI started in Hanoi, where I was born and raised. As an undergraduate at Hanoi University of Science and Technology, I spent three years at the BKAI lab forecasting river levels and air quality from cheap, noisy sensors. Along the way I won a hackathon with a flood-forecasting platform. At the VinUni-Illinois Smart Health Center I built a pill-detection system that uses prescriptions as extra context. An internship at AIST in Japan, on multimodal learning with missing data, set the question I brought with me to Illinois.

Today I work end to end. I design methods (contrastive and self-supervised objectives, uncertainty estimation, diffusion distillation, RL fine-tuning) and evaluations that measure more than a single headline score. I also handle the engineering to train and evaluate large models on GPU clusters. My papers have appeared at IJCAI, ACL Findings and ICDM, with ACCV 2026 to come. Since 2025 I've been a Research Scientist Intern at Cantina Labs, working on image and video diffusion models.

I'm looking for Summer 2027 research internships in multimodal learning, generative models and trustworthy AI. Email is the fastest way to reach me, and I'm always up for a research chat.
