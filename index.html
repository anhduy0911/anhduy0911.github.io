Hi, I'm Duy! 👋

I'm a third-year PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, advised by Prof. Minh N. Do and co-advised by Prof. Khoa D. Doan (VinUniversity).

I grew up in Hanoi, where one of my first research projects was forecasting river levels from noisy sensor data. It left me with a question I keep coming back to: what happens to a model when the data stops being clean? Today I chase it in multimodal models that survive missing inputs, in medical VLMs that know when not to answer, and in image generators that try to keep every person recognizable, even in a crowd of 20.

I love the full loop of research: a fresh idea, the experiments that prove it wrong, and the day it finally works. If that sounds like your team, let's talk!
